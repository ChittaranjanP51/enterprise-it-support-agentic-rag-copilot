# VPN and Password Policy (sample)

## Resetting your VPN password
1. Open the self-service portal at https://selfservice.example.com.
2. Choose "Reset VPN credentials" and verify with your MFA code.
3. Wait 5 minutes for the change to sync, then reconnect the VPN client.

If the reset fails, open a ticket with the Service Desk (ext. 4000) and include your employee ID.

## Password policy
- Minimum 14 characters, with upper/lower case, a digit and a symbol.
- Passwords expire every 90 days; the last 10 passwords cannot be reused.
- Five failed attempts lock the account for 15 minutes.

## Laptop issues
- Wi-Fi not connecting: forget the "Corp-WiFi" network, rejoin, and sign in with your SSO account.
- Slow laptop: restart, check for pending updates, and free at least 15 GB of disk space.
