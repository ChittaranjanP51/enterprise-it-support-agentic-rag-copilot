# Acme Corp HR Policy (sample placeholder, ~100 employees)

## Working hours and remote work
- Core hours are 10:00-16:00 local time; standard week is 40 hours.
- Employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week with manager approval.
- Remote employees must use the company VPN and an encrypted company laptop.

## Leave
- Annual leave: 22 paid days per year, accrued monthly. Up to 5 unused days carry over.
- Sick leave: 10 paid days per year; a doctor's note is required after 3 consecutive days.
- Parental leave: 16 weeks fully paid for primary caregivers, 6 weeks for secondary caregivers.
- Request leave in the HR portal at least 2 weeks ahead for periods longer than 3 days.

## Equipment and IT
- New hires receive a laptop, monitor and headset on day one; IT sets up accounts before arrival.
- Lost or stolen devices must be reported to IT Support (ext. 4000) within 2 hours.
- Personal devices may access email only through the approved mobile management app.

## Expenses and travel
- Submit expense reports within 30 days with receipts; claims over $500 need director approval.
- Flights over 6 hours may be booked in premium economy.

## Conduct and onboarding
- Annual security awareness training is mandatory for all 100 employees.
- Probation period is 3 months; performance reviews happen every June and December.
- Report harassment or policy concerns to hr@acme.example or the anonymous ethics line.

## Contacts
- HR: hr@acme.example, ext. 4100. IT Support: it@acme.example, ext. 4000.
